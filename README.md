## Hello 🌻
<br>
My name is Nita, and I am a PhD Candidate at the University of Michigan. 

I currently work on research related to how social and environmental exposures impact differences in later life DNA methylation.  

You can read the first aim of my dissertation [here]([https://posit.co/download/rstudio-desktop/](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.24.26358676v1)) and the code used to carry out the research can be found in this [repo](https://github.com/bakulskilab/EarlyLife_DNAm_HRS).

Along with coding in R, I have experience using SAS, HTML, and CSS. However, I am always seeking to expand my programming knowledge.
